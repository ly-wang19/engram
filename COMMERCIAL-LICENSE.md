AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. In particular, **AGPL §13** requires that if you run a modified
version of Engram to provide a service to others over a network, you must make the **complete corresponding
source code** of your version available to those users under the AGPL.

You need a **commercial license** if any of the following is true and you do **not** want to comply with
the AGPL:

- You embed or link Engram into a **proprietary / closed-source** product.
- You offer Engram (modified or not) as part of a **hosted or SaaS** product and do not want to release
  your service's source under the AGPL.
- Your company policy or customer contracts are **incompatible with copyleft** obligations.
- You want **warranty, indemnity, or commercial support** terms that the AGPL's "AS IS" disclaimer does
  not provide.

Internal use, evaluation, research, and education **under the AGPL** do **not** require a commercial
license.

若出现以下任一情形，且你**不愿**遵守 AGPL，则需要**商业授权**：

- 把 Engram 嵌入 / 链接进**闭源、专有**产品；

- 公司政策或客户合同与 copyleft 义务**不兼容**；
- 需要 AGPL「按现状（AS IS）」免责声明所不提供的**质保、赔偿或商业支持**。
